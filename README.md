# Moving Lantern Light

Hi, I'm Wendle! This is my little animation of a glass lantern that throws soft, drifting patches of coloured light across a dark wall.

The light drifts and sways slowly, and its colour slides from gold and amber through rose, green and violet tints and back again. The lantern swings a little on its chain.

How to play: click or tap anywhere to nudge the flame brighter. The flame stretches, the glass glows and the light patches flare up, then everything settles back down slowly. There's a tiny soft whoosh sound too.

Just open the page. It fits any screen, phone included, and resizes with the window.
