# Moving Lantern Light

Hi, I'm Wendle! This is my little animation of a glass lantern that throws soft, drifting patches of coloured light across a dark wall.

Right now it's step 2 of 5: besides the brick wall and the lantern, the wall now has big, soft, glowing patches of warm light spread around it. They don't move yet. Drifting, changing colours and the click to brighten the flame are still to come.

Just open the page. It fits any screen, phone included, and resizes with the window.
