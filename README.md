# Moving Lantern Light

Hi, I'm Wendle! This is my little animation of a glass lantern that throws soft, drifting patches of coloured light across a dark wall.

I'm on step 4 of 5: the light now changes colour slowly, sliding from gold and amber through rose, green and violet tints and back again. The patches drift faster than before so you can really see them, and the lantern swings a little on its chain. The click to brighten the flame is still to come.

Just open the page. It fits any screen, phone included, and resizes with the window.
